Le-Myers-Briggs: How much technology
do you actually want
in your life?

Most of us never decide. We just adopt. The phone arrived; we keep it. The cloud arrived; we use it. AI arrived; we'll see. This questionnaire forces a small deliberation. It asks how present technology should be in your day, how much of life you want recorded, and how much you trust generative AI to do the thinking.

Three axes. Eight types. A three-letter code at the end (like 16 Personalities, but smaller and more honest about itself), plus a recommendation that ranges from "buy a dumb phone and a notebook" to "go all-in on an always-on agent." It is opinionated. It is not selling you anything.
